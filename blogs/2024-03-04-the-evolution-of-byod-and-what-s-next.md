The first time I brought my BlackBerry to work in the early 2000s, it sat in my desk drawer until I had to head to the airport for a tradeshow and customer event. Bring Your Own Device (BYOD) has come a long way since then. In fact, it has transformed the business landscape, forever changing the way we communicate and work.
